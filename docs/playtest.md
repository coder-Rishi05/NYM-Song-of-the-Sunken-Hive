# Playtest & Pacing Notes — NYM: Song of the Sunken Hive

This document records the pacing budget, playtest routing, zone durations, and balance considerations for first-time runs versus speedruns.

---

## 2. Speedrun & Critical Path Routing (~20–25 Minutes)

For experienced players skipping optional lore and backtracking:

1. **Cradle of Ash** (`cradle_01` → `cradle_04`) — ~1.5 min.
2. **The Rootway** (`rootway_01` → `rootway_04`) — pass through to Sporefall.
3. **Sporefall Hollows** (`sporefall_01` → `sporefall_05`) — acquire **Skitter Dash**, take shortcut back to `rootway_03`.
4. **Drowned Cistern** (`cistern_01` → `cistern_05`) — beat **Moltguard**, acquire **Cling**, take shortcut back to `rootway_03`.
5. **Ember Warrens** (`ember_01` → `ember_04`) — collect **Wingbeat** and **Wisp Bolt**.
6. **Frozen Reliquary** (`reliquary_01` → `reliquary_03`) — defeat **Warden Molt**, unsealing the great doors.
7. **The Choir Deep** (`choir_01` → `choir_02`) — defeat **The Choir Mother** and trigger ending.

## 3. Pacing & Accessibility Tuning

- **Forgiving Checkpointing**: Every major transition zone sits at most 2–3 rooms away from a resting bench.
- **Soul Economy Recovery**: Enemies respawn on bench rest, ensuring players low on Soul or Mask HP can safely farm nearby Crawlids before retrying difficult boss encounters.
- **Visual & Audio Accessibility**:
  - Screen shake intensity adjustable in Options.
  - White flash reduction toggle for damage and parry effects.
  - Independent master, music, and sound effects volume channels.
